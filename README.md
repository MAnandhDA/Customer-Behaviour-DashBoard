# Customer-Behaviour-DashBoard
Fashion Customers Behaviour Analysis


# 📌 Project Overview

The **Customer Behaviour Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, spending habits, product performance, and customer satisfaction.

The dashboard transforms raw retail shopping data into meaningful business insights, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions for improving sales, customer retention, and marketing strategies.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of customer transaction data every day. Without proper analytics, it becomes difficult to identify purchasing trends, understand customer preferences, and optimize business performance.

This dashboard addresses the following business questions:

| Business Question | Business Insight |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Which product category generates the highest revenue? | **Clothing** is the highest revenue-generating category, contributing significantly to overall sales. |
| Which customers purchase more frequently? | Customers with active subscriptions and frequent purchases in the Clothing category show higher purchase frequency. |
| What is the average customer spending? | Customers spend an average of **$59.80** per purchase. |
| How satisfied are customers? | Customers have an average review rating of **3.73 / 5**, indicating generally positive satisfaction. |
| Which customer segment should marketing focus on? | High-value Clothing customers and subscribed customers provide the greatest opportunity for targeted marketing campaigns. |
